Before You Book

The four questions everyone asks

Is it safe?

Screened, lot-tested, documented

Every patient is reviewed by a physician-led team first. Tissue comes only from accredited banks with sterility, viability and potency testing on every lot, and consent is in plain language, including what is still unknown. Side effects are possible with any procedure and are discussed before you decide.

Is it for me?

Only if a clinician says so

Candidacy is decided at your consultation, from your history and, where they matter, your labs. If you are not a fit you will hear that first, along with where to go instead.

What does it cost?

A written quote, financing if you want it

Pricing is explained before you commit, with monthly plans through a financing partner, subject to approval. No countdown timers, no pressure.

What happens on the day?

A nurse practitioner, in your home

Your visit is scheduled around you. A licensed nurse practitioner, under physician oversight, administers the treatment at home or at your office, explains aftercare and books your follow-up.

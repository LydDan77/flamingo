What we will not promise

Regenerative medicine attracts big claims. Here is where we draw the line, so you can decide with clear eyes.

We will not

  • {{icon:x}}Promise a cure, a guarantee, or a specific result. Responses vary from person to person.
  • {{icon:x}}Describe stem cell or exosome therapy as FDA-approved. It is not, and we say so in writing.
  • {{icon:x}}Treat anyone without a clinician review of their history, and labs where they matter.
  • {{icon:x}}Pressure you with countdown timers or "last spots". Decide at your own pace.
  • {{icon:x}}Hide what it costs. Pricing and financing are explained before you commit to anything.

We will

  • {{icon:check}}Tell you honestly if you are not a candidate, and point you somewhere better.
  • {{icon:check}}Put a physician's name on your plan and a licensed nurse practitioner in your home.
  • {{icon:check}}Source only from accredited tissue banks, with lot documentation available on request.
  • {{icon:check}}Walk you through consent in plain language, including what is still unknown.
  • {{icon:check}}Keep one record and one team across everything we do with you.

Who this is not for

  • {{icon:info}}Anyone looking for a cure or a guaranteed outcome.
  • {{icon:info}}People with an active infection, an uncontrolled medical condition, or cancer under active treatment. Please speak with your treating physician first.
  • {{icon:info}}Those who would rather skip labs, follow-ups, and an honest conversation about candidacy.

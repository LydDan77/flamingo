The Process

How stem cell therapy works

{{icon:clipboard-check}}01

A licensed practitioner reviews your condition and medical history and determines whether stem cell therapy is appropriate for you.

FAQ

Stem cell therapy questions answered

How much does stem cell therapy cost?{{icon:plus}}
Because every program is personalized to your condition, the protocol, and the number of areas addressed, pricing is determined individually and walked through clearly during your consultation.
What results can I expect?{{icon:plus}}
Clinical outcomes vary by condition and patient. Many people with musculoskeletal conditions report meaningful improvement in pain and function, but results are not guaranteed. We set realistic, individualized expectations during your consultation.
What are the benefits of stem cell therapy?{{icon:plus}}
Potential benefits include reduced inflammation, support for tissue repair, improved joint function, and longer-lasting relief by addressing the source rather than masking symptoms. Wharton's Jelly MSCs carry a low rejection risk and do not require immunosuppressants.
Can stem cell therapy help with back pain?{{icon:plus}}
It may. Protocols for back pain target disc degeneration, facet joint inflammation, and soft-tissue damage. Candidacy is assessed during your consultation, and we'll tell you honestly if you're not a fit.
Is stem cell therapy safe?{{icon:plus}}
Our protocols use rigorously tested Wharton's Jelly MSCs from accredited tissue banks. The therapy is well tolerated by most patients. Every patient goes through a clinical screening process before treatment.

Your back, your neck, and where we honestly draw the line

Standing at the sink. Turning your head to reverse the car. Sitting through a dinner. Backs and necks are where we say no most often, so this page is as much about who we send elsewhere as what we offer.

Is this you?

The back that sets the schedule

People come to us with backs and necks like these.

  1. Standing in one place, at the sink or in a queue, is harder than walking.
  2. Turning your head to check a blind spot has become a whole-body turn.
  3. Sitting through a meal or a film ends with a careful stand-up.
  4. Imaging shows wear in the small joints of the spine, or discs that have lost height.
  5. You have pain that shoots down a leg or an arm, and you are not sure what it means.
What we do

What we can, and cannot, offer a back or neck

The treatment, in plain words

For wear in the small joints of the spine, the facet joints, a licensed nurse practitioner can inject cells from donated, screened umbilical cord tissue, sometimes with exosomes, around those joints. The aim is to support repair and calm inflammation. Spine work is often image-guided and arranged at a partner facility rather than at home; the clinician tells you which applies.

Who it tends to suit

Wear-related back and neck pain without nerve involvement, in people who have tried the basics, movement, strengthening, time, and want a non-surgical option. Candidacy is a careful clinical decision made on your video call, with your imaging.

What we will not promise

Anything for pain that shoots down a limb, numbness, weakness, or changes in bladder or bowel control. Those signs need a spine specialist, promptly, and we will say so on the call and help you get there. Stem cell and exosome treatments are not FDA-approved for the spine and are investigational; results vary and we promise none.

Questions

What people with back trouble ask us

Anything else, ask on the call. Get in touch.

Why are you more cautious about backs?{{icon:plus}}
Because the spine carries the nerves, and because wear-related back pain often improves with movement and time. We treat a narrow set of back and neck problems and send the rest to specialists. That is not a sales technique; it is the reason you can trust the yes when you get one.
Is a disc problem the same as joint wear?{{icon:plus}}
No. Discs that have lost height, bulging discs and nerve compression are different problems with different options. The clinician on your call will tell you which you have and whether we are the right people.
Can this be done at home?{{icon:plus}}
Sometimes, for the small joints of the spine. Often it is image-guided at a partner facility. Either way, the follow-ups are with the same team and the price is in writing first.

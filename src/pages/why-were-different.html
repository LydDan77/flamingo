Why {{site.name}} is different

Regenerative medicine attracts big promises. Here is how we work instead: tissue from accredited banks, licensed clinicians, a plan built for you, and plain answers at every step.

Our Principles

Eight things we hold ourselves to

From where the tissue comes from to the follow-up call, here is how we work. The treatments themselves are not FDA-approved, and are investigational for most uses. Results vary.

01{{icon:flask}}

Tissue From Accredited Banks

The cells are Wharton's Jelly cells from donated umbilical cord tissue, supplied by accredited tissue banks.

02{{icon:shield-check}}

Every Lot Tested

Every lot is tested for sterility, viability, and potency before it reaches a patient. The tissue bank documents the chain of custody, and you can ask to see it.

03{{icon:users}}

Licensed Clinicians, Physician-Led

We do not sell products online. A licensed nurse practitioner gives your treatment, and a named Medical Director oversees every patient.

04{{icon:heart}}

A Plan Built for You

Each plan is built around your health history, your goals and, where they matter, your labs. You see it in writing before you decide.

05{{icon:book-open}}

Education-First Approach

Before any treatment, you will know what is being used, why, and what to expect, including what is still unknown.

06{{icon:zap}}

Current With the Research

Our clinical team keeps reading the research as it is published. We tell you what it shows, and what it does not.

07{{icon:map-pin}}

Care That Comes to You

Regenerative treatment happens in your home. {{#if any:hormones,peptides,nad}}Other programs run by video call.{{/if}}{{#unless any:hormones,peptides,nad}}Consultations happen by video call.{{/unless}} One team coordinates all of it.

08{{icon:award}}

Honest follow-up

We follow how each patient does and set honest expectations. What we learn, not anecdotes, guides how the process is refined.

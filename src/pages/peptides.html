FAQ

Common questions about peptide therapy online

How do I get peptides prescribed online?{{icon:plus}}
Book a free consultation, complete a telehealth intake with a licensed clinician, and receive your prescription from a licensed compounding pharmacy, all without leaving home.
What should I look for in an online peptide clinic?{{icon:plus}}
A licensed clinician who reviews your labs and coordinates your peptide protocol with your hormones and NAD+, rather than a storefront that just ships a product. Here, the same medical team manages all of it.
How long does peptide therapy take to work?{{icon:plus}}
Many patients notice changes within 4–8 weeks depending on the peptide and protocol. Recovery peptides can show results sooner; GLP-1 programs typically show meaningful weight changes over 8–12 weeks. Individual results vary.
Is peptide therapy safe?{{icon:plus}}
Peptide therapy prescribed and monitored by a licensed clinician is generally well tolerated. All protocols are reviewed by the medical team and dispensed by accredited compounding pharmacies.

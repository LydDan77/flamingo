04 · Website & technology stack

What this website is built on

A fast static site with no frameworks to maintain, five switchable design versions, and every client-specific detail in one configuration file.

{{icon:palette}}

Five design versions

Dark (athlete), Light (clinical), Warm (older adults), Bright (bold) and Elite (premium). Switchable per visitor; photos, fonts and motion adapt per version.

Switch with the Design control
{{icon:zap}}

Static build, zero dependencies

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript generated by a small Node build. No framework upgrades, no plugins, no database to patch.

Hosting: Vercel, global CDN
{{icon:gauge}}

Performance

Only the active version's fonts load, the hero photo is preloaded, images are responsive WebP, assets are minified and cached for a year with fingerprinted names.

Typical page: under 300 KB
{{icon:file-text}}

One configuration file

Brand name, contact details, hours, booking link, navigation, footer and optional modules live in a single file. Edit once; every page updates on the next build.

site.config.js
{{icon:image}}

Photography & product renders

Licensed stock processed into responsive sets with per-version swaps, plus transparent product renders for vials and droppers.

Replace by dropping files in one folder
{{icon:shield-check}}

Compliance-aware copy

No claims, no fabricated testimonials or figures: every client-pending item is a labelled placeholder with FDA, telehealth and compounding disclaimers in place.

Counsel review before launch

07 · Illustrative planning tool

Explore a path to break-even

Editable assumptions estimate gross modeled revenue less the platform fee and entered monthly costs. Product and clinical costs are not included, so this is not take-home cash or a payback forecast.

$75,000 modeled revenue − $2,497 platform fee − $0 entered costs = $72,503 before product and clinical costs

$75,000Gross modeled revenue, before product / clinical costs
$2,497Platform fee + entered overhead
$72,503Modeled amount after fee + overhead, before product / clinical costs
$870,036Annualized, before product / clinical costs

This simplified model treats treatment value as gross modeled revenue and subtracts the required monthly platform fee and entered overhead. It does not subtract product or clinical costs and does not represent net cash, profit or a reliable payback estimate. Treatment value is a planning input, not a guarantee of collections. Additional product purchases, launch costs, financing, taxes, ramp-up, retention, collection timing, regulatory requirements and unentered operating costs are excluded.

08 · Launch readiness

What the partner needs to provide or resolve

{{icon:building}}01

Buyer responsibilities

Secure the office; identify business / MSO structure and operating staff; provide approved business details and content; complete required onboarding.

{{icon:stethoscope}}02

Provider readiness

Confirm the provider arrangement and the professional-corporation-to-MSO connection. Acceptance before premises and provider arrangements are identified is case by case.

{{icon:file-text}}03

Website handoff

Brand name, logo, contact details, team names and credentials, verified testimonials, statistics, financing partner and reviewed legal copy replace the labelled placeholders.

{{icon:check-circle}}04

Activation

Go-live follows completion of agreed buyer, provider and digital checklists. A launch date is not guaranteed before readiness criteria are met.

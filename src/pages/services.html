The Science

What is Wharton's Jelly?

Found within the umbilical cord, Wharton's Jelly is a gel-like tissue rich in mesenchymal stem cells. These cells are known for their regenerative signaling and their ability to differentiate into cell types involved in repairing tissue and calming inflammation.

That is why Wharton's Jelly is studied as an option for chronic joint and soft-tissue pain: it is aimed at supporting repair from within rather than masking symptoms.

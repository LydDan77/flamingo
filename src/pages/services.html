The Science

What is Wharton's Jelly?

Wharton's Jelly is the soft tissue inside the umbilical cord. It holds many of the cells used in this treatment. Researchers study these cells for the signals they send to the tissue around them.

That is why it is being studied for long-standing joint and soft-tissue pain. The research is still early. It does not tell us who will be helped, or by how much.

Why Consider It

Why consider Wharton's Jelly stem cell therapy?

People ask about this when a joint or an old injury has hurt for a long time. Here is what the treatment is, and what we can and cannot say about it. It is not FDA-approved, and is investigational for most uses. Results vary.

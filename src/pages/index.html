Regenerative Medicine

Your joints shouldn't be a surgery countdown

Stem cell and exosome therapy, delivered in your home by a licensed nurse practitioner under physician oversight. A non-surgical option for joints and soft tissue. It is not FDA-approved, and it is investigational for most uses.

  • Pain or reduced mobility in joints and soft tissue
  • Post-injury recovery and soft-tissue wear
  • Patients weighing options before surgery
  • Active adults whose joints are holding them back

Not every joint is a candidate. If yours isn't, we'll tell you on the video call and send you back to your specialist with a clearer picture.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Plain answers to the questions we hear most. Still have questions? Get in touch.

What is stem cell therapy?{{icon:plus}}
It uses cells from donated, screened umbilical cord tissue, not cells from your own body. A licensed nurse practitioner gives them by injection. Researchers are studying how these cells signal the body's own repair and inflammation. The treatment is not FDA-approved, and it is investigational for most uses. Results vary.
Why Wharton's Jelly?{{icon:plus}}
Wharton's Jelly is the soft tissue inside the umbilical cord. Cords are donated after healthy births, and an accredited tissue bank screens and tests the tissue. Nothing is taken from your own body, so there is no harvesting procedure. Whether cells from one source do more than cells from another is still being studied, and we do not claim that they do.
Is this safe?{{icon:plus}}
Every patient is screened by a licensed clinician first. Side effects are possible with any procedure, and we go through them with you before you decide. These treatments are not FDA-approved, and they are investigational for most uses. Results vary.
What kinds of wellness goals do people come to you for?{{icon:plus}}
{{#if any:nad,hormones,peptides}}Joint discomfort, post-injury recovery, fatigue, inflammation, longevity, and performance are the most common.{{/if}}{{#unless any:nad,hormones,peptides}}Joint discomfort, recovery after an injury, and questions before surgery are the most common.{{/unless}} We don't make specific medical claims. We work with each patient to understand their goals and match them with the right plan and clinician.
What happens during a consultation?{{icon:plus}}
It is a video call with a licensed clinician, not a sales pitch. You go over your health history and what you are dealing with. Bring your imaging if you have it. You get an honest yes, no, or "we need more information". If it is a yes, you get a written plan and price, and you decide in your own time.
How soon do people notice a difference?{{icon:plus}}
It depends on the person and the plan. Any change tends to be gradual, over weeks or months, and some people notice little change. We say so before you decide. Results vary.
Do you work with other clinicians and clinics?{{icon:plus}}
Yes. If you are a clinician or run a clinic and want to work with us, our For providers & partners page explains how it works.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our therapies and partnerships. Still have questions? Get in touch.

What is stem cell therapy?{{icon:plus}}
Stem cell therapy uses mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs), sourced from ethically donated umbilical cord tissue rather than from your own body, to support the body's natural repair processes. The cells are introduced by injection and are studied for how they signal the body's inflammatory and tissue-repair pathways.
Why Wharton's Jelly MSCs specifically?{{icon:plus}}
Wharton's Jelly, found in umbilical cord tissue, is one of the richest known sources of high-quality MSCs. Because these cells come from newborn tissue, they're younger and more active, and they carry a lower risk of immune rejection than adult-derived cells.
Is this safe?{{icon:plus}}
Our protocols use rigorously tested products sourced from accredited tissue banks. Wharton's Jelly-derived MSCs are well tolerated by most people and do not require immunosuppressants. Every patient goes through a clinical screening process first.
What kinds of wellness goals do people come to you for?{{icon:plus}}
Joint discomfort, post-injury recovery, fatigue, inflammation, longevity, and performance are the most common. We don't make specific medical claims. We work with each patient to understand their goals and match them with the right protocol and clinician.
What happens during a consultation?{{icon:plus}}
Your consultation is a conversation, not a sales pitch. We go over your health history, listen to what you're dealing with, and walk you through which protocols might make sense. From there, you're connected to a licensed clinician who handles the clinical side.
How soon do people notice a difference?{{icon:plus}}
It depends on the person and the protocol. Some patients notice changes within a few weeks; for others it unfolds over several months. We set realistic expectations from the start, and individual results vary.
Do you work with gyms, clinics, or other health professionals?{{icon:plus}}
Yes. We have a partnership program for clinicians, gyms, recovery centers, and physical therapists. Visit our For Providers page for how it works.

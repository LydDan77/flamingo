FAQ

Questions about the concierge model

What is concierge regenerative medicine?{{icon:plus}}
Concierge regenerative medicine means your care is delivered in your home by a licensed clinician. No clinic visit, no waiting room. Here, it also means one physician-led team coordinates all your protocols: regenerative therapy and {{site.programsPhrase}}.
Who oversees my care?{{icon:plus}}
Every patient is overseen by a named Medical Director. Nurse practitioners deliver day-to-day care under that clinical supervision. Clinician names and credentials will be listed here once provided by the client.
What's the difference between this model and a regular telehealth company?{{icon:plus}}
Most telehealth companies sell one product in a silo. We coordinate everything under one chart, so your {{#if any:hormones,peptides}}weight-loss program{{/if}}{{#unless any:hormones,peptides}}longevity protocol{{/unless}} can be built around your joint regeneration plan instead of competing with it.
Do I need to visit a clinic?{{icon:plus}}
No. Regenerative therapies are delivered in your home by a licensed nurse practitioner. Telehealth services ({{site.programsPhrase}}) are handled entirely online: consultation, prescription, and delivery.
What areas do you serve?{{icon:plus}}
Service area details will be added once confirmed by the client. Ask during your consultation about availability in your area.

FAQ

NAD+ therapy questions answered

What is NAD+ therapy?{{icon:plus}}
NAD+ therapy delivers nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide to support mitochondrial energy production, DNA repair, and cellular longevity. We offer clinician-supervised injection and sublingual protocols tailored to your labs and goals.
What is the difference between NAD+ injection and sublingual NAD+?{{icon:plus}}
Injection delivers NAD+ with strong absorption and is a convenient at-home option. Sublingual NAD+ is absorbed under the tongue and is the most convenient delivery method. Your clinician recommends the right form based on your goals and labs.
How long does NAD+ therapy take to work?{{icon:plus}}
Many patients notice improved energy and mental clarity within 1–2 weeks. Deeper cellular benefits build over 4–8 weeks of consistent use. Individual results vary.
Is NAD+ therapy safe?{{icon:plus}}
NAD+ therapy is generally well tolerated. Some patients notice mild, temporary flushing or warmth. Your clinician supervises all protocols and adjusts dosing based on your response.
Can I get NAD+ therapy at home?{{icon:plus}}
Yes. Both injection and sublingual options let you start NAD+ therapy from home, supervised by the clinical team. No clinic visit required.

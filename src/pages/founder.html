Why {{site.name}} Exists

The origin story, in the founder's words.

{{#if sample}}
Sample

I started {{site.name}} in {{site.founded}} because of what I kept seeing. People with a sore knee or hip drove a long way, sat in a waiting room, and went home without a clear answer or a clear price.

So we turned it around. A nurse practitioner comes to your home. A physician reads your history and reviews your plan before any visit is booked.

The price goes in writing before you decide. We do not promise results. And if this is not a fit for you, we say so and tell you who to see next.

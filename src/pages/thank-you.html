What happens next

Three steps, in plain words

{{icon:phone}}01

Your video call

About thirty minutes with a licensed clinician. We ask about your knee, hip, shoulder or back, your health, and what you want back.

{{icon:clipboard-check}}02

An honest answer, in writing

Yes, no, or we need more information. If yes, a written plan with the full price. A quote is never a commitment.

{{icon:home}}03

Treatment at home

A licensed nurse practitioner comes to you. A physician oversees the plan. Follow-ups are with the same team.

Get ready

Five things that make the call count

None of them are required. All of them help the clinician give you a straight answer.

  1. Your imaging, if you have it: X-ray or MRI reports, or the images themselves.
  2. A list of what you take, including supplements and injections you have had in the joint.
  3. What you want back: the stairs, the garden, sleeping on that side, the walk that got shorter.
  4. If a surgeon has given you a date, bring it. It changes the advice.
  5. Your questions, written down. The price, the follow-ups, what we will not promise. Ask all of them.

FAQ

Provider partnership questions

What kind of providers do you partner with?{{icon:plus}}
Licensed nurse practitioners, physician assistants, and independent clinics who want to offer regenerative medicine and telehealth services without building the infrastructure from scratch.
Do I need my own malpractice insurance?{{icon:plus}}
Yes. All partnered clinicians maintain their own malpractice coverage. The medical group provides clinical oversight and protocol supervision, but each NP or PA operates under their own license and coverage.
How does the revenue share work?{{icon:plus}}
Revenue share terms are discussed during the partnership application process. There is no upfront franchise fee. Details will be finalized by the client. {{icon:pen}} Client to confirm
What training and protocols do you provide?{{icon:plus}}
All partnered clinicians receive access to a vetted protocol library covering regenerative medicine, peptide therapy, hormone optimization, GLP-1, and NAD+. Continuing education and clinical support are included.
What telehealth platform do you use?{{icon:plus}}
A white-labeled telehealth platform handles intake, e-prescribing, compounding pharmacy handoff, and the patient portal. Platform details will be added once confirmed by the client.

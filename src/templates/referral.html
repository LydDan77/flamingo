Is this approved?{{icon:plus}}
No. Stem cell and exosome products are not FDA-approved to treat joints and are considered investigational for most uses. We say so up front, and we will not tell you otherwise. What we can say is who tends to be a candidate, what the treatment involves, and what the research does and does not show.
Will it hurt, and how long does it take?{{icon:plus}}
The visit is about an hour, most of it preparation and aftercare instructions. The injection itself takes a few minutes and feels like any injection into a joint. Mild soreness for a day or two is common. You can usually go about a light day afterward.
What if I am not a candidate?{{icon:plus}}
Then we tell you, on the call, and we send a note to {{page.ref.short}} with your permission. A meaningful share of the people we talk to are better served by their specialist, and we would rather say so than treat someone who is unlikely to benefit.

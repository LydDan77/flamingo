# Patient test kit

Three people over 55, twenty minutes each, one week. This ends every argument in the room about what "clear" means, and it is the professional, internal, organised version of trusting your gut.

## Who to ask

Three people over 55 who are not in marketing or healthcare and have never seen the site. A neighbour, a parent's friend, someone from church or the gym. Pay them with lunch. One should have a knee, hip or shoulder that bothers them; one should be a phone-only internet user; one should wear reading glasses.

## Set-up

Send this message, and nothing else:

> My friend's doctor recommended this site to her for her knee. Could you look at it on your phone for five minutes as if it were you, then answer four questions for me? https://flamingo-regenerative-revival.vercel.app/three/

For the referred-patient path, send `/from-dr-reyes` instead and change "her doctor" to "Dr. Reyes".

Do not sit beside them. Do not explain anything. Let them scroll alone, then ask.

## The four questions

Ask in this order, write their exact words, and do not help.

1. In your own words, what is this?
2. Is it for you? Why or why not?
3. What would you do next, if anything?
4. What does it cost?

Then two follow-ups:

5. Was there a word or a sentence you did not understand? Which?
6. Did anything make you trust it more? Anything make you trust it less?

## What a pass looks like

| Question | Pass |
| --- | --- |
| What is this? | They say "stem cell treatment for joints, at home, from a nurse" or close to it, without the word "protocol" |
| Is it for you? | They name their own knee, hip or shoulder, or say honestly that they are not the type it describes |
| What next? | "Book the free call" or "call the number", and they can find the button in under ten seconds |
| What does it cost? | They quote the range, or say "they tell you on the call, in writing" |
| Word they did not understand | None, or one that is already on the banned list in `docs/PATIENT-WORDS.md` |
| Trust | They mention the "what we will not promise" section, the named clinician, or the price in writing |

Two of three on every row is a pass. One fail on the same row from two people is a fix, not an opinion.

## Record it

One line per person per question in a shared doc. Quote them exactly. Then one paragraph: what you changed because of it. That paragraph is what you show colleagues; it is worth more than any design review.

## Repeat

Every time the home page or a condition page changes materially, run it again with three new people. Twenty minutes each. The site is for them, not for us.

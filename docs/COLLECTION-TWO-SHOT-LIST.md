# Collection Two: imagery shot list (Adobe Stock)

Purpose: a new photographic look for each of the five Round 2 themes. Seventeen images in total; the hero image for each theme is marked. Search strings are written for Adobe Stock's search box; refine with the filters noted.

## Rules for every pick

- **Outcomes, not procedures.** People moving, resting, working, laughing. No needles, vials in hands, gloves, operating rooms, or anyone in a hospital gown.
- **Real people, realistic ages.** 35 to 70, mixed, no stock-photo grins. Diverse casting across the set.
- **No before-and-after, no bodies presented as "results".**
- **Licence.** Standard licence is enough for web use. Skip anything marked "Editorial use only". Avoid visible brand logos, car badges and recognisable landmarks.
- **Files.** Largest JPG offered (at least 2400 px on the long edge). Name them `<theme>-<subject>.jpg` (for example `atelier-portrait-man.jpg`) and put them in one Drive folder.
- **Filters that help:** Orientation as noted, "People: with people / without people", "Colour" where noted, exclude "Generative AI" (Adobe's filter) unless noted; AI imagery of people is a trust risk for a medical site.

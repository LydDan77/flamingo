# Patient words

The reader is in pain, paying out of pocket, and afraid of being conned. Clarity is the kindness and the conversion at the same time. Every page a patient reads is written in the patient's words, and `node tools/patient-words.js` measures it: on each patient page, body and situation words must outnumber the banned words below. The build prints the one-line summary every time.

## Who we write for

Carol, 64, right knee, pain on the stairs. Her chiropractor said "look at this, I think it could help your knee." She opens the link on her phone at nine at night. She wants four things, in this order: is this for me, is it safe and honest, what does it cost, what do I do next. Everything else is secondary.

## Say this, not that

| Instead of | Say |
| --- | --- |
| telehealth | a video call |
| protocol | a plan, the treatment |
| biologic, MSC, mesenchymal stromal cells | the cells, cells from donated umbilical cord tissue |
| exosomes (first mention) | the signals cells send each other, then "exosomes" |
| vesicle | skip it |
| lab-guided | built around your labs |
| candidacy | whether you are a candidate, whether this is a fit |
| investigational (alone) | not FDA-approved, and investigational for most uses |
| your regenerative health journey | nothing; cut the sentence |
| elevate, holistic, modality, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, synergy, leverage, seamless, empower, unlock, transform | nothing; these are the words every AI draft uses |
| optimize, optimization | get back to, improve, feel like yourself |

## Use her words

Stairs. Chairs. Socks. The garden. Sleeping on that side. Reversing the car. Grandchildren on the floor. The walk that got shorter. The surgeon's date. The price. Who answers the phone.

## Structure every patient page the same way

1. See yourself first: situations in her words, before any science.
2. Feel safe second: a named clinician, "what we will not promise", not FDA-approved said plainly.
3. The truthful cost of waiting, which for wear is simply that it does not reverse on its own.
4. One small step: a free video call, with a first name attached.
5. Exactly what happens after: steps, price in writing, follow-ups with the same team.

## Where the science goes

Under the page, as "the science, if you want it", with links to the journal. Never above the fold. Provider pages (`for-providers.html`, the program pages) may use clinical vocabulary; they are not patient pages and the lint does not score them.

## Compliance never bends to plain language

Plain words still say: not FDA-approved, investigational for most uses, results vary, candidacy decided by a clinician, if you are not a fit we say so. The California §24185 notice stays on the regenerative pages. No testimonial promises an outcome.
